What we are setting out to do

We are building programmes in three areas. Details will be added here as each one begins.

01

Education

Too many young people leave school early or never get the support that makes learning stick. Our education work will focus on practical help for learners and the people who teach them.

02

Leadership and mentorship

Talent is common and guidance is not. We want young people to have someone ahead of them on the road who can show them how the next step is taken.

03

Access to opportunity

Scholarships, grants and trainings exist, but they are hard to find and harder to trust. We want to make real opportunities easier to reach.

How we will report

When a programme starts, it will have its own entry here with what it sets out to do, what it costs and what happened. Its spending will appear on the public ledger.
